\section{Results}
\outline{The evidence is early and mostly simulated. Only 9 of 90 studies used real multi-institutional data. Centralised training was at least as accurate as FL in 17 of 24 head-to-head comparisons. The common claim that FL is comparable or better is overstated. Privacy was asserted, not measured. Few studies ran a real attack or reported a usable privacy budget. Ophthalmology-specific gaps: device heterogeneity and federated foundation-model pre-training.}
